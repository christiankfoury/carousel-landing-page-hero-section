## AI Log — Carousel Equipment Lending Hero

- **Research & discovery — ChatGPT:** Used ChatGPT to research Carousel's website, understand its product offering, and identify equipment lenders as the target audience for the landing page.

- **Messaging & visual direction — ChatGPT:** Developed the hero headline, supporting copy, and overall design direction, focusing on Carousel's existing typography, warm neutral palette, and a clear "Book a demo" conversion goal.

- **Implementation planning — ChatGPT:** Structured the development requirements into prompts covering the frontend architecture, component organization, fictional application data, and the contractor-to-lender user journey.

- **Frontend development — Codex:** Used Codex to implement the landing page in React, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS, with reusable components for the hero, mobile application, and lending dashboard.

- **Product visualization — Codex:** Generated the layered phone and dashboard interfaces to illustrate the transition from contractor application submission to lender review.

- **Visual refinement — Codex:** The initial product visualization included dotted arrows ending in a checkmark. I felt these added unnecessary visual clutter, so I instructed Codex to remove them for a cleaner, more focused composition.

- **Mobile refinement — Codex:** Although the initial mobile layout was technically responsive, the content alignment felt visually unbalanced. I prompted Codex to center the content and refine the mobile composition to improve its visual appeal.

- **Final review — ChatGPT & Codex:** Used iterative prompting and visual inspection to refine spacing, typography, and layout consistency while keeping the final result aligned with Carousel's branding and the assignment requirements.
